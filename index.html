The 12-point licence

Every driving licence in Bangladesh carries 12 demerit points. Nine types of offences — including speeding, reckless driving, drunk driving, illegal overtaking and signal violations — deduct points. When the points reach zero, the licence is cancelled. Offences marked with the demerit badge below are demerit-point offences.

If you are fined — what to do

  1. Stay calm and ask the officer for the case slip (challan) with the offence and the amount written on it.
  2. Note the officer’s name and badge number and the vehicle number on the slip.
  3. Pay only against the official slip — at the designated bank or BRTA office — and keep the receipt.
  4. If you believe the fine is wrong, you can contest it in court; keep the slip as evidence.
  5. Never pay cash on the spot without a slip.

Where the numbers come from

Fine amounts are the maximum fines under the Road Transport Act 2018 (in force 1 November 2019), as reported by The Daily Star and The Business Standard (TBS) in November 2019. The amount actually imposed can be lower — the officer or the court decides. Verify current rules with BRTA.
